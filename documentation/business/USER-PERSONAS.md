# USER PERSONAS

## Overview

User personas are fictional characters that represent the different user types within a targeted demographic that might use the application. They help guide design and development decisions by providing insights into user needs, behaviors, and goals.

## User Personas

### 1. Movie Enthusiast

- **Name**: Alex
- **Age**: 28
- **Occupation**: Graphic Designer
- **Tech Savvy**: High
- **Goals**:
  - Discover new movies and genres.
  - Keep track of movies watched and create a personalized watchlist.
  - Share movie recommendations with friends.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Difficulty finding movies that match personal taste.
  - Overwhelmed by the number of streaming options available.
- **Usage Scenario**:
  - Alex uses the app daily to browse new releases and add movies to their watchlist. They often share their favorite finds on social media.

### 2. Casual Viewer

- **Name**: Jamie
- **Age**: 35
- **Occupation**: Teacher
- **Tech Savvy**: Medium
- **Goals**:
  - Find family-friendly movies for weekend movie nights.
  - Get recommendations based on previous viewing history.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Limited time to search for movies.
  - Needs a simple interface to navigate.
- **Usage Scenario**:
  - Jamie opens the app on Friday evenings to quickly find a movie for family night, relying on curated lists and recommendations.

### 3. Film Student

- **Name**: Sam
- **Age**: 22
- **Occupation**: Film Studies Student
- **Tech Savvy**: High
- **Goals**:
  - Research films for academic projects.
  - Analyze different genres and directors.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Needs access to a wide range of films, including classics and indie films.
  - Requires detailed information about films, such as cast, crew, and reviews.
- **Usage Scenario**:
  - Sam uses the app to search for specific films, read reviews, and compile lists for class assignments.

### 4. Parent

- **Name**: Taylor
- **Age**: 40
- **Occupation**: Marketing Manager
- **Tech Savvy**: Medium
- **Goals**:
  - Find age-appropriate movies for children.
  - Create a watchlist for family movie nights.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Concerned about the content of movies for children.
  - Needs a way to filter out unsuitable content easily.
- **Usage Scenario**:
  - Taylor uses the app to browse family-friendly categories and set up a watchlist for the kids.

### 5. Movie Critic

- **Name**: Jordan
- **Age**: 30
- **Occupation**: Freelance Writer
- **Tech Savvy**: High
- **Goals**:
  - Stay updated on the latest releases and trends in cinema.
  - Write reviews and share insights on social media.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Needs quick access to new releases and screening information.
  - Requires a platform to publish reviews and engage with followers.
- **Usage Scenario**:
  - Jordan uses the app to track upcoming releases and write reviews, often sharing them on their blog and social media.

## Conclusion

Understanding these user personas will help in tailoring the application's features and user experience to meet the diverse needs of its audience. By focusing on these personas, the development team can prioritize features that enhance user satisfaction and engagement.
